Car Link privacy policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Car Link ("the app") is made by Ziv Ginsberg, an individual developer. This page explains what the app does with your data. Short version: everything stays on your phone unless you turn on a feature that needs the internet, and there is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

What the app does

Car Link reads your car through an OBD2 adapter (Bluetooth or Wi-Fi) and shows the readings, fault codes and your drives on your phone. Trips, readings, routes, recordings and settings are stored only on your phone.

What is never collected

  • No account and no sign in.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK, no crash reporting SDK.
  • Google Play may collect crash and performance statistics for apps it distributes; these are aggregated by Google and shown to the developer without identifying you.

Location

If "Record my route" is on and you allow location access, the app records your route while a trip is running. If you also turn on "Start automatically", a trip can start by itself while the app is closed; to draw the map of those trips the app asks, after an explanation on screen, for location "Allow all the time" (background location). You can say Not now, and those trips are then saved without a map. Location is used only while a trip is recording, never while the app is idle. Routes are stored on your phone and shown on your own trip maps. They are sent nowhere, except the start and end points if you turned on "Name my start and end places" (see above).

Phone motion

If you turn on "Use phone motion to detect trip end" (off by default) and allow Physical activity, the app asks Google Play services whether the phone is in a vehicle, walking, running, on a bicycle or still. It uses this only to notice that you walked away from a stopped car, to end the trip at the stop or ask you first. Only the current activity is kept, in memory, while the app is connected. No motion history is stored, and the app sends it nowhere. Activity recognition runs inside Google Play services on your phone and is covered by Google's privacy policy. Turn the setting off, or revoke Physical activity in Android settings, to stop it.

Google Maps directions

If you turn on "Read Google Maps directions" (off by default) and allow Car Link in the system Notification access screen, the app reads only the Google Maps navigation notification to show the next turn, the arrival time and the distance and time left. Notifications from every other app are ignored. What it reads is kept in memory only while you navigate: it is never stored or logged, and it is sent nowhere except the destination name, to Nominatim, while the Drive map is open (see above). Turn the setting off, or remove Car Link from Notification access, to stop it.

Drive map

The Drive map shows your live location, the route driven since you opened it, the posted speed limit and a suggested speed (never above the limit). The route on this screen is kept in memory and is gone when you close it. It never sends anything to your MQTT server.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to talk to your OBD2 adapter. It scans for devices only when you tap Scan and it does not use Bluetooth to find your location.

Sharing and exports

You can share a diagnostics report, a CSV or a GeoJSON file, or a trip story. These are created only when you tap share, and go only where you send them. Diagnostics reports never include passwords or server addresses, and they mask the VIN serial number unless you turn off "Hide VIN and identifiers" before sharing.

Debug log

The app keeps a diagnostic log on your phone (on by default, Diagnostics: turn off "Keep a debug log"). It holds connection events and trip timings and distances, never passwords, server addresses or your VIN. It leaves the phone only if you share it.

Your control and deletion

  • Delete a trip ("Delete this trip") or all trips ("Delete all trips"), or use "Forget this car" to remove the saved car.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone.
  • The app does not back up its data to the cloud.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete or hand over; if you have a question, contact us below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the app starts sending anything new, this page and the in-app settings will say so before it happens. The effective date at the top changes with every update.
